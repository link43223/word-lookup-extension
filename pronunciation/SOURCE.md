# Bundled American English pronunciations

`en_US/*.txt` comes from [open-dict-data/ipa-dict](https://github.com/open-dict-data/ipa-dict), `data/en_US.txt`, retrieved 2026-09-30 (source SHA-256: `2AF6F154A5C363275F052D1F85ACEDEF38ED185CA9745AA4314BE77F6B70DE67`). Its US pronunciations are based on [cmudict-ipa](https://github.com/lingz/cmudict-ipa) and [syllabify](https://github.com/kylebgorman/syllabify), as documented by the source project. The wordlist is split mechanically by first letter, with the original tab-delimited UTF-8 lines unchanged. Fifteen entries beginning with an apostrophe are omitted because this extension does not recognize apostrophe-initial words.
